# Changelog

What changed in each version of Pomelo. Updates arrive through the Microsoft Store.

## 1.2.0, October 2026

- Documents, slides and spreadsheets, without Office. Word documents (DOCX, DOC), RTF, plain text, Markdown and web pages convert to PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, HTML, EPUB, PNG and JPG. PowerPoint decks convert to PDF, PNG and JPG. Excel workbooks, CSV and TSV convert to PDF, XLSX, CSV, TSV and ODS.
- Images: Annotate… draws arrows, boxes, circles, pen and highlighter strokes and text on a photo. Copy text reads the text in an image with the text recognition built into Windows and puts it on the clipboard.
- Video: convert to WebM and to an animated GIF. Speed… makes a copy from 0.25× to 4× with the voice at its pitch, and Frames… saves the frames you pick as PNG.
- Unzip also extracts TAR and GZ files.
- Settings has its sections in a bar on the left. You can put the wedges of each tab in your order, hide the ones you don't use and give the wheel an accent colour, and set the quality of JPG, WEBP, AVIF and HEIC, MP4 up to 4K and in HEVC, the audio bitrates, Apple Lossless and the page size of PDFs.

## 1.1.0, 24 October 2026

- Wedges that open a window: Compress… for images, audio and video, to a size or a percentage; Crop…, Edit… and Collage… for images; Organize… for the pages of a PDF; Trim…, Normalize…, Channels…, Bleep… and Visualizer… for audio; Trim…, Crop…, Split… and Join… for video. They replace the fixed compress, resize and mono wedges of 1.0.
- Metadata… changes or removes the metadata of images, PDFs and audio files without encoding them again.
- Audio converts to OGG, Opus, AIFF and WMA, and reads OGG, Opus and AIFF.
- New actions: Terminal opens a terminal in the folder, and Unzip extracts ZIP files.

## 1.0.0, 22 October 2026

- First release. Drag files in File Explorer, tap Shift, Ctrl or Alt, and drop them on a wedge of the wheel: 99 conversions between 22 formats of images, PDFs, audio and video, 18 tools and 6 file actions.
