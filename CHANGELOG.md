# Changelog

What changed in each version of Pomelo. Updates arrive through the Microsoft Store.

## 1.6.0, November 2026

- Pomelo speaks eight languages: English, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Italian, French, Spanish, Portuguese and German. It follows the language of Windows, and Settings > Appearance > Language picks another one.
- Settings > Appearance > Font gives the wheel and the tab names above it one of ten fonts.

## 1.5.0, November 2026

- New kinds of files: subtitles (SRT, VTT, ASS, LRC), web fonts (WOFF, WOFF2), contacts (VCF), calendars (ICS), GPS tracks (GPX, KML, KMZ, GeoJSON) and 3D models (STL, OBJ, 3MF), each with its own wheel. Images also read JFIF, SVG, camera RAW, Photoshop files, TGA, DDS, EXR and HDR, and audio reads M4B, AMR and 3GA. In all, 739 conversions between 89 formats.
- Images: Convert to sRGB, Auto enhance, Social sizes, Split into tiles, Fit for email, Copy image, Add background, Animation…, Contact sheet, Turn upright, Scan… and Combine into TIFF. Text in an image or a PDF table goes to XLSX or CSV.
- PDF: Redact…, Sign…, Page numbers…, Flatten form, Remove blank pages, Fronts and backs, Pages per sheet…, Extract images, PDF/A copy and Turn pages upright, and PDF to TXT and TIFF.
- Audio: Clean up voice, Tempo…, Fit for email, Split…, Remove pauses, Chapters… and Mix….
- Video: Watermark…, Contact sheet, Slides to PDF, Fast MP4, Replace sound, Extract subtitles, Burn subtitles…, Stabilize, Vertical…, Fit for email and Convert to WEBP.
- Documents and spreadsheets: Compare, Clean up, Merge, Stack rows and Fix encoding….
- Actions: ZIP with password…, Unblock…, Checksum, New folder…, Dates from photos, Copy as table, Sort into folders…, Compare, Takeout dates and Find duplicates…. Unzip also reads RAR and 7z.
- Recipes run two to five steps from one wedge, made in Settings > Wedges. Watch folders run a wedge on every file that arrives in a folder. Settings > General can save the copies in a Pomelo folder, without a suffix, or move the originals to the Recycle Bin after Convert.
- The wheel takes a dropped folder, files dragged from a phone, a camera or a ZIP, and the Windows Share menu. With an option in Settings > General, a drop on the centre of the wheel repeats the last tool.
- Once the 20 free files are used, there are 2 free files a day from the next day.

## 1.4.0, October 2026

- Video: Trim… becomes Edit…, a window with tracks that cuts a video into pieces, takes parts out or leaves gaps, changes the volume and the speed, fades in and out, adds music, removes the pauses of a talk, crops and turns the picture, and saves it all as one copy with one encoding. Undo goes back 100 steps, and Play, Cut, Delete, Undo, Redo and Zoom each have a key. With "Fast, no re-encoding" on, a copy that only cuts is ready in seconds, without encoding the video again. The copies end in `-edited`.
- Speed…, Crop…, Rotate 90° and Mute stay on the video wheel for a single change.
- Fixed: closed windows stayed in memory. Tool windows were held by the window frame, the app's theme and the video preview, the Settings and Details windows by their own handlers, and the audio windows by their player.

## 1.3.0, October 2026

- Images: Resize… makes a copy at a size or a percentage. ICO turns an image into a Windows icon. Watermark… puts a text over a photo, diagonally in the middle, in the bottom right corner or tiled across it, at the size, opacity and colour you choose.
- PDF: Protect… adds a password and Remove password… takes it off; the password is never written to disk. Make searchable reads scanned pages with the text recognition built into Windows, so their text can be found and copied. Watermark… puts the same kind of text on every page.
- Audio: Fade… fades a recording in and out, and Join… puts recordings one after another in a single file.
- Video: Rotate 90° turns a video a quarter turn clockwise.
- Rename… renames files in one go with find and replace, text before and after, numbers and dates, and shows the new names before it changes anything.
- The progress card shows a dot, yellow while the job runs, green when it is done and red when it fails or is cancelled, and the percentage next to its bar. It takes the accent, opacity and size of the wheel.
- The names and icons in the wedges are larger, and the preview in Settings shows the wheel size you choose.
- To make room, on tabs you have not arranged yourself Combine into PDF, Rotate 90° for PDFs, Visualizer…, Frames… and Terminal now start off the wheel. Settings > Wedges puts any of them back.

## 1.2.0, October 2026

- Documents, slides and spreadsheets, without Office. Word documents (DOCX, DOC), RTF, plain text, Markdown and web pages convert to PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, HTML, EPUB, PNG and JPG. PowerPoint decks convert to PDF, PNG and JPG. Excel workbooks, CSV and TSV convert to PDF, XLSX, CSV, TSV and ODS.
- Images: Annotate… draws arrows, boxes, circles, pen and highlighter strokes and text on a photo. Copy text reads the text in an image with the text recognition built into Windows and puts it on the clipboard.
- Video: convert to WebM and to an animated GIF. Speed… makes a copy from 0.25× to 4× with the voice at its pitch, and Frames… saves the frames you pick as PNG.
- Unzip also extracts TAR and GZ files.
- Settings has its sections in a bar on the left. You can put the wedges of each tab in your order, hide the ones you don't use and give the wheel an accent colour, and set the quality of JPG, WEBP, AVIF and HEIC, MP4 up to 4K and in HEVC, the audio bitrates, Apple Lossless and the page size of PDFs.

## 1.1.0, 24 October 2026

- Wedges that open a window: Compress… for images, audio and video, to a size or a percentage; Crop…, Edit… and Collage… for images; Organize… for the pages of a PDF; Trim…, Normalize…, Channels…, Bleep… and Visualizer… for audio; Trim…, Crop…, Split… and Join… for video. They replace the fixed compress, resize and mono wedges of 1.0.
- Metadata… changes or removes the metadata of images, PDFs and audio files without encoding them again.
- Audio converts to OGG, Opus, AIFF and WMA, and reads OGG, Opus and AIFF.
- New actions: Terminal opens a terminal in the folder, and Unzip extracts ZIP files.

## 1.0.0, 22 October 2026

- First release. Drag files in File Explorer, tap Shift, Ctrl or Alt, and drop them on a wedge of the wheel: 99 conversions between 22 formats of images, PDFs, audio and video, 18 tools and 6 file actions.
